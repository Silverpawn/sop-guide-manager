Schema & Usability Audit

📐 Section A: The "Naive Colleague" 10-Second Rule (Core Philosophy) Enforced by DESIGN_GUIDE.md & validate-guides.js
1. Why am I here?

Every Playbook Overview starts with a 2-sentence plain-English goal explaining what the guide helps you achieve in the real world.

2. How do I use this?

Never stamp loud compliance badges like MANDATORY or OPTIONAL across stage cards. Show a simple "Ways to Use This Guide" banner instead.

3. What do I do next?

Maximum 4 high-level stages on any Journey Map, aligned 1-to-1 with the 4 universal navigation tabs.

Section B: Strict Card Density Standard (Max 4 Elements)

  1. Plain-English Title: Max 7 words, starting with an action verb (e.g., "1. Score Your Site Together in a Pair").
  2. One-Sentence Summary: Plain-English explanation of what happens in that stage (no unexplained rules).
  3. Who & How Long: Single clean line (e.g., "Programme Manager + Core Team Partner • 90 mins"). No double labels.
  4. What You Finish With: Human outcome (e.g., "Agreed scorecard for 28 site practices"). Never show .csv / .json or Input → Output: on overview cards.

Section D & E: SOP Step, Visual & Brand Standards

  • ≤20-Word Rule & Active Verb Start: Every step action must be ≤20 words and start with a direct action verb.
  • Zero Raw Acronyms & Point-of-Use Delivery: Explain rules in Step 1 where needed; put filing rules at the final step where saving happens.
  • Flexible 3-Mode Visuals (M365 Ready): Supports image (Ctrl+V Snipping Tool Base64 + hotspot), svg, or none.
  • Siemens Energy Brand Tokens: Header #1B1534 → #4D217A, Petrol #009999, Bright Purple CTA #8A00E5, Ice-Blue cards #EAF4F8.
