Schema & Usability Audit

📐 Section A: Stage 7 Architecture, Intranet Tool Links & Demo Playbook Standard Enforced by DESIGN_GUIDE.md & validate-guides.js
1. Business Need + Gateway Capability Rule

"The goal is not to chase a maturity score; the goal is to improve the business. However, foundational Gateway capabilities (★) must be strengthened to unlock and sustain that business performance."

2. Dynamic N-Stage Journey Maps (Locked 6-Stage Navigator)

Journey Maps support N stages to match the real operational sequence. For Navigator (PLB-001), the order is strictly locked: 1. Maturity Assessment → 2. Analysis → 3. Voice of the Customer (VOC) → 4. Navigator Workshop → 5. Charter → 6. Action Plan.

3. Direct Routing, Zero Database IDs & Zero Version Numbers

Tab 1 is the single transparent process overview. Clicking a stage or sidebar guide routes directly to the Step-by-Step Guide—never through an intermediate PRC-xxx cover page. Never display internal IDs (PRC-001, SOP-001) or software version numbers (central live web links always open the latest version).

Section B: Corporate Intranet Tool Links & Demo Playbook Rule

  1. Official Corporate Intranet Tool Links & Strict Tool Boundary Rule: Always store and render clickable Launch Live Tool ↗ buttons (target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer") on Stage Cards, Kit Bags, and Tool Reference drawers:
    • Navigator Process (PLB-001) Uses Navigator Toolkit / GS Lean Maturity Only: Runs entirely in https://megblcntpvc72.ad101.siemens-energy.net/navigator-toolkit/ and https://megblcntpvc72.ad101.siemens-energy.net/gs-lean-maturity/. NEVER link PM Maturity Hub or its sub-tools (Metro Journey & 4 Pillars, Score Explainer, Strategy Builder, Simulator & CSV Fixer) inside PLB-001.
    • PM Maturity Hub Is a Separate System (PLB-PMHUB): https://megblcntpvc72.ad101.siemens-energy.net/pm-maturity-hub/ has its own distinct tools and does not participate in the Navigator Workshop; keep its guides and tools in its dedicated Playbook (PLB-PMHUB).
  2. Neutral Demo Playbook Standard (PLB-DEMO): Maintain a neutral, everyday-life example playbook ("Demo Example: The Office Brew Run") in the Playbook Switcher so stakeholders can evaluate the platform UX and authoring features without getting distracted by internal process debates.
  3. Role Ownership Rule (LPM Owns the Process, Core Team is Optional Support): The Local Programme Manager (LPM) is the primary owner and driver of all 6 stages of the Navigator Process—including leading the Stage 4 Navigator Workshop and Stage 5 Charter with their Site Leadership Team. After the first-time Maturity Assessment calibration, the Lean Core Team is always optional support / coaching, never mandatory.
  4. Two-Step Scoring Progression: 1st Time: LPM + Core Team Partner (learn the tool). Ongoing: LPM + Site Leadership Team (capability self-assessment, not an audit; Core Team optional).

Section D & E: Target Action Focus (2–3 Key Actions) & Step Rules

  • Focus on 2–3 Key Actions (Max 5 Ceiling): Focus on 2 to 3 key actions at a time (with a hard ceiling of 5) so teams finish what they start instead of building a giant wishlist.
  • ≤20-Word Rule & Active Verb Start: Every SOP step action must be ≤20 words and start with a direct action verb.
  • Flexible 3-Mode Visuals (M365 Ready): Supports image (Ctrl+V Snipping Tool Base64 + hotspot), svg, or none.
  • Official Siemens Energy Wordmark & Tokens: Stacked two-line geometric SVG wordmark (#009999 Petrol + #FFFFFF White on #1B1534 → #4D217A gradient), Bright Purple CTA #8A00E5.
