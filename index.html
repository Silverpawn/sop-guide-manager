Schema & Usability Audit

📐 Section A: The "Naive Colleague" 10-Second Rule & Flexible N-Stage Journeys Enforced by DESIGN_GUIDE.md & validate-guides.js
1. Why am I here & Golden Principle

Every Playbook starts with a plain-English goal and a high-contrast Golden Principle box (e.g., "The goal isn't to improve the maturity score. The goal is to improve the business.").

2. Zero Compliance Stamps or Tech Plumbing

Never stamp MANDATORY / OPTIONAL or Input → Output: Raw scores.csv / .json on stage cards. Use a helpful "Ways to Use This Guide" banner instead.

3. 4 Universal App Tabs + Flexible N Stages

Keep the 4 top navigation tabs fixed while allowing Tab 1's Journey Map to render as many connected stage cards as the real process requires (e.g., 6 stages for Navigator, 4 for Daily Management).

Section B: Clean 4-Element Stage Card Layout

  1. Stage Number & Plain-English Title: e.g., "Stage 1: Establish the Baseline (Assess)".
  2. What Happens: 1–2 plain-English sentences explaining what happens in the real world.
  3. Who Is Involved & Tool Used: Clear human roles and clickable tool badge.
  4. What You Finish With: Tangible outcome (e.g., "Focused view of 2–3 priority capability gaps to develop").

Section D & E: Focus on 2–3 Key Actions & Step Rules

  • Focus on 2–3 Key Actions (Max 5 Hard Cap): Recommend 2 to 3 focused capability actions at a time so teams actually finish what they start.
  • ≤20-Word Rule & Active Verb Start: Every SOP step action must be ≤20 words and start with a direct action verb.
  • Flexible 3-Mode Visuals (M365 Ready): Supports image (Ctrl+V Snipping Tool Base64 + hotspot), svg, or none.
  • Siemens Energy Brand Tokens: Header #1B1534 → #4D217A, Petrol #009999, Bright Purple CTA #8A00E5, Ice-Blue cards #EAF4F8.

Section C: Progressive Disclosure & "The Red Pen Lexicon" (Mandatory Replacements)

"Self / Pair Calibration & Scoring" "Establish the Baseline (Assess)"
"Gateway Cap & Gap Diagnosis" "Understand the Gaps (Analyse)"
"Leader Interviews (VOC)" "Understand the Business Need (Voice of the Customer)"
"Prioritise the 'Vital Few' (Max 5)" "Focus on 2–3 Key Actions at a Time (Max 5 Hard Cap)"
